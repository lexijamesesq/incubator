# Product Brief: [Initiative Name]

*A short brief that lets product, design and engineering agree on the problem and the direction before detailed work starts. Replace every bracketed prompt, and delete this line.*

---

## Initiative Hypothesis

One sentence, in this form:

We believe [this initiative] will [specific outcome for users] by [approach].

---

## Problem & Opportunity

One paragraph. Say who struggles, what they do today and why that falls short, what they do as a result, and what changes once the problem is solved.

---

## Strategic Anchors

Connect the work to the priorities it serves. Name each priority and say how this work moves it; be plain about a weak connection. Answer four questions, a short paragraph each:

- How does this advance our product goals?
- How does this advance our design goals?
- What larger outcome does it contribute to?
- What happens if we do not do it?

---

## Jobs to be Done

What people must be able to do once the work is complete. Group by the kind of user, and describe outcomes, not features.

- When [user] wants to [goal], they need to be able to: [capabilities]

---
