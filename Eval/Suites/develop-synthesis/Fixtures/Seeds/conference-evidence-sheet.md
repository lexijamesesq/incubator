## Conference Evidence Sheet

**Core insight:** Teachers build a page of evidence for each parent conference by hand, because Benchmark cannot show one student's results as one page.
**Source:** Inbox capture (2026-10-12)
**Initial strategic connection:** Advances Measure 1c by giving districts a reason to keep Benchmark alongside Coursebook, since no platform produces a conference-ready sheet.

### Original Capture

Conference week. A teacher showed me her preparation: for each of 112 students she opens the Benchmark tracker, takes a screenshot of that student's row, pastes it into a document, and types two sentences underneath. It took her most of two weekends. Benchmark has every result she is pasting. It has no view that is about one student and fits on one page a parent can hold. Nobody else does this either, so we would be first. What goes on the page: every standard, or the three that matter this term? Does the sheet go home, or stay in the room?
