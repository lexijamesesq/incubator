## Assessment Load Calendar

**Core insight:** Every teacher sets Coursebook due dates alone, so a student can find five pieces of work due on one day and no adult saw it coming.
**Source:** Conversation with a Coursebook solutions engineer (2026-10-19)
**Initial strategic connection:** Extends Fewer Tabs by showing a year group's deadlines in one view, using the school timetable Coursebook already holds.

### Original Capture

A head of year told our engineer that the week before reports her Year 10s had six deadlines on the Thursday. Each teacher had set a reasonable date. Coursebook holds every one of those dates and the timetable, and shows no one the pile-up. What if setting a due date showed how loaded that day already is for the class?
