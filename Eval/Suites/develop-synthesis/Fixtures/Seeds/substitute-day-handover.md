## Substitute Day Handover

**Core insight:** A substitute teacher walks into a class with a paper folder, because Coursebook has no way to hand one day of one class to someone who is not on staff.
**Source:** Slack thread in the Coursebook field-notes channel (2026-09-21)
**Initial strategic connection:** Supports No Lost Week by keeping a lesson usable on a day its teacher is out.

### Original Capture

Field note. A teacher told me she was up at five with a fever writing notes for her substitute: which room, which period, where the worksheet is, which two students must not sit together. All of it except the seating is already in Coursebook. The substitute cannot see any of it because she has no login, so the notes get emailed to the school office and printed. The class watched a film. This happens in every school we visit and we have never built anything for the person covering the lesson. What is the smallest thing a substitute needs on the day? Can someone without a staff account be let in for one day and no longer?
