# Research Handoff: conference-evidence-sheet

**Date:** 2026-11-10
**Idea:** Conference Evidence Sheet
**Seed core insight:** Teachers build a page of evidence for each parent conference by hand, because Benchmark cannot show one student's results as one page.
**Seed strategic connection:** Advances Measure 1c by giving districts a reason to keep Benchmark alongside Coursebook, since no platform produces a conference-ready sheet.

---

## Strategic Context (Stream A)

### Product Strategy — Direct

- The Entered Once pillar reads: "a learner's evidence should read as one account to anyone entitled to see it." It names the parent conference as the occasion where today's record fails most visibly. ([Larkmoor Product Strategy 2026, Entered Once](https://example.com/larkmoor-product-strategy-2026-one-record))

### Design Strategy — Adjacent

- Fewer Tabs covers the screenshot-and-paste routine, though the routine happens twice a year and not daily.

### OKR Connections

- Measure 1c (districts renewing with two or more Larkmoor products): Adjacent. Nine districts dropped Benchmark and kept Coursebook in 2025–26; two cited parent reporting in exit interviews. ([Larkmoor exit interviews 2025–26](https://example.com/larkmoor-exit-interviews-2026))

### NPS / Customer Evidence

- Larkmoor Benchmark (April–September 2026): 27 comments about conference preparation, most describing screenshots pasted into a document. ([Benchmark NPS — September 2026 tab](https://example.com/benchmark-nps))
- **Key finding:** Teachers raise this unprompted, and the comments cluster around the two conference rounds.

### Shared Research Baseline (within TTL)

- Basin teachers spend a median 4.5 hours preparing for each conference round, and there are two rounds a year, in October and March. (high) ([Fernhollow Institute, Teacher Time Study 2025](https://example.com/fernhollow-teacher-time-study-2025))

## Market Intelligence (Stream B — EdTech SME)

### Market Fit: Moderate

Buyers expect it and will not pay for it. A per-student summary for families is turning into something an assessment product is assumed to have.

### Competitive Positioning

| Competitor | Relevant Capability | Comparison |
| ------------ | --------------------- | ------------ |
| Corvid Classroom (registry) | "Conference Cards": a one-page summary per student, shipped March 2026, translated into six home languages | Behind |
| Pennywhistle Assess (registry) | Class-level report only; no per-student page | Differentiation |
| Mossgate Labs (discovered) | Nothing comparable | Differentiation |

**Positioning narrative:** The seed's claim that no platform produces a conference-ready sheet is wrong. Corvid shipped Conference Cards in March 2026 with translation ([Corvid Classroom release notes, March 2026](https://example.com/corvid-release-notes-2026-03)). Larkmoor is behind, and nothing in the research suggests a version that would put it ahead. This is a catch-up feature. The case for it is the teachers' time, not a lead.

### Technology Risk: Low

Every figure on the sheet is already in Benchmark. The work is layout, print, and translation.

### Strategic Timing

The clock is the school calendar. A sheet that is not ready for the October 2027 round is a sheet teachers do without for another year, in a year when Corvid's customers have one.

## Educator Perspective (Stream D)

### Adoption Assessment: High

It replaces two weekends of screenshots.

### Pain Point Validity

Felt twice a year and remembered all year. One hundred and twelve students, one screenshot each, two sentences each.

### Adoption Barriers

A sheet the teacher cannot edit will not be handed to a parent. A low result printed with no context starts the conference badly, and teachers know which three results they want on the page and which they would leave off.

### Champion Scenario

The evening before conferences, a Year 6 teacher opens thirty sheets, each already showing the term's standards with the most recent result beside each. She removes one line on four of them, adds a sentence to nine, and prints the set in twenty minutes.

## Divergent Reframing (Stream E)

### The Disruptive Angle: The student presents the sheet

Let the student choose what goes on it and lead the conference. The sheet turns from a report about a child into something the child prepared.

*This is a reframing candidate, not evidence. The synthesis agent decides whether it strengthens the strategic compound.*
