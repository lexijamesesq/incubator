## The finding a passing artifact reaches

The numbers are free to vary. Two points are not. First, the estimate cannot be confident: the price input is assumed, and the artifact says so. Second, the card leaves open whether the coordinator has a budget at all, so a passing artifact shows the result both ways, as a paid capability and as a capability that only protects renewals, and does not pick the larger one as its headline.

## What would fail here

- Using sixty dollars per supported student as the spend input without flagging it.
- A SOM above 22% of districts with no stated basis.
- Adding regional colleges to SAM without noting that nobody has validated the segment.
