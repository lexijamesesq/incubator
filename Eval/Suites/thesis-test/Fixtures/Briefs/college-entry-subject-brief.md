## 1. Strategic Claims

### Thesis A: The placement office

- **Thesis:** Enter through the placement office. Every college tests incoming students to place them in courses, the office that does it has a budget and a deadline every intake, and Benchmark is a testing product. Win placement and the rest of the college follows.
- **Why now:** two colleges asked a Larkmoor account lead about Benchmark for placement in 2026.

### Thesis B: The instructor's own course

- **Thesis:** Enter through individual instructors with a free tier of Coursebook. College instructors choose their own tools; once enough of them run courses in Coursebook, the college buys what its staff already use.
- **Why now:** Corvid Classroom opened a free instructor tier in 2024 and Larkmoor has no answer to it.

### Thesis C: The dual-enrolment door

- **Thesis:** Enter through dual enrolment. School students already take college courses for credit, their districts already run Coursebook, and the college instructor teaching them has to work somewhere. Make that place Coursebook, and the college's first experience of Larkmoor is a course that works.
- **Why now:** the Basin Education Authority's Early College Credit scheme funds dual-enrolment places from the 2027 intake.

The three differ in wedge, buyer, and surface: A sells testing to an office, B gives a course tool to an individual, C extends a district's existing licence across a partnership.
