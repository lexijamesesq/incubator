**Status:** Hypothesis under test. Lenses should validate, challenge, or break it. Do not treat as established truth.

## 1. Strategic Claim

### Thesis A: The Plan Feed

- **Thesis:** Benchmark reads each student's accommodations straight from the district's support-plan system and applies them with no teacher involved, and it is sold to learning-support coordinators on the audit record that results. The plan system is the only trustworthy source, and the coordinator is the only person with an obligation.
- **Why now:** audits start January 2027; two coordinators raised it unprompted in the September renewals review; no competitor applies a plan without the teacher.
- **What it combines:** a feed from the plan system, automatic application on every check, and an audit report for the coordinator.

## 2. Break-Condition Table

### Thesis A: The Plan Feed

| Lens | Break condition (if X, this thesis weakens/breaks) |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| edtech-sme | If the system that holds most districts' plans cannot or will not release them to another product, the thesis breaks, because a feed with no source is not a feed. |
| tam-estimate | If coordinators have an obligation and no software budget of their own, the thesis weakens, because the named buyer cannot buy (willingness-to-pay observables). |
| educator-sme | If teachers will not let an accommodation be applied that they did not see and approve, the thesis weakens, because "no teacher involved" is the part they reject (protagonist workflow-reality). |
