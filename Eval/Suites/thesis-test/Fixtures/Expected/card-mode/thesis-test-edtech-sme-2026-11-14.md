## Roll-up

- **Thesis A: BREAKS.** Load-bearing break: the plans cannot be fed. One source says the holder will not release them and a second says the law restricts the transfer whoever holds them. Assumption-to-knowledge ratio: 0 of 1 conditions unknown or conditional.

Evidence: Tamarisk's terms forbid third-party retrieval, and the Charter requires per-student guardian consent for a transfer. Interpretation: the thesis fails at its source and not at its destination, because nothing found here weakens the case for applying accommodations on a check; what fails is the claim that they can arrive from the plan system.

Interpretation: the one competitor to move has gone round the plan system, which is consistent with the wall being real.
