# Thesis Test — Educator SME lens: accommodations-on-classroom-checks

**Brief:** `Research/accommodations-on-classroom-checks/thesis-brief.md`
**Charge:** test mode. Attempt to falsify Thesis A on this lens's two break conditions. Named attack: protagonist workflow-reality.
**Carried forward (not re-derived):** the first-pass account of the minutes before a check opens. **Re-run:** teacher acceptance when the teacher is removed from the decision.

## Verdicts

### Thesis A: The Plan Feed

| Break condition | Verdict | Qualifier | Cited evidence |
|-----------------|---------|-----------|----------------|
| Teachers will not let an accommodation be applied that they did not see and approve | CONDITIONAL HOLD | Holds if the teacher sees what will be applied before the check opens and can correct it. Does not hold for "no teacher involved" as the brief words it. A plan is reviewed once a year; a teacher knows by October that a student no longer wants the read-aloud in front of the class. | 26 of 34 teachers had forgotten an accommodation at least once that term, and in the same interviews 21 of 34 said they adjust what a plan specifies for particular tasks ([Larkmoor district interviews, June 2026](https://example.com/larkmoor-district-interviews-2026-06)). Support Flags was ignored in 9 of 12 pilot schools because it left the work with the teacher ([Support Flags retrospective, December 2024](https://example.com/support-flags-retrospective)). |
| Learning-outcome: the wedge changes nothing a learner or teacher does inside a Larkmoor product during teaching, and lands only on an office's purchasing or record-keeping | HOLDS | — | The effect is on the student sitting the check: a longer timer, a read-aloud that is on. That is a learner inside a Benchmark classroom check. Two bases: the same interviews record 26 of 34 teachers forgetting, so the change in what students experience is real and frequent; and the Authority's audit guidance defines an applied accommodation as a change to the conditions under which the student completes the task ([Basin Education Authority, Learner Support Statute audit guidance, April 2026](https://example.com/bea-learner-support-audit-guidance)). The audit report is a second effect and is not the one this verdict rests on. |

## Roll-up

- **Thesis A: CONDITIONAL HOLD.** Load-bearing break: none on this lens; the condition is teacher sight of what is applied. Assumption-to-knowledge ratio: 1 of 2 conditions unknown or conditional.

Evidence: most teachers forget an accommodation at some point, and most also adjust plans for particular tasks. Interpretation: automatic application is wanted and unattended application is not, because the teacher holds knowledge about the student that is newer than the plan.
